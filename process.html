How a project
becomes a home.

01
Weeks 1 — 4

The Conversation

Every project begins with a long, unhurried conversation — at the site if possible, at the studio if not. We discuss the house, but we also discuss your mornings, your dinners, the books on your nightstand.

Deliverables

  • Site walk-through & survey
  • Brief & vision document
  • Phase plan & investment range
  • Letter of agreement
Notes — First site visit
02
Weeks 4 — 12

The Concept

A material direction is drafted, defended, and refined — usually across three meetings. Floor plans, joinery sketches, palette boards, the first lighting plot. Nothing is purchased yet; everything is decided.

Deliverables

  • Floor plans & elevations
  • Material palette & samples
  • Lighting & electrical plot
  • Preliminary furniture concept
Sample board — Material direction
03
Months 3 — 9

The Build

Documents are issued. Trades are selected from a vetted bench. The studio attends every weekly site meeting and resolves every detail in person — joinery reveals, tile layouts, plaster colour mocks.

Deliverables

  • Construction documents
  • Trade procurement & contracts
  • Weekly on-site oversight
  • Custom joinery & millwork
On-site — Joinery, week 14

Between every phase — a pause. We sit with the work, look at it in different light, and return to it the following week.

04
Months 8 — 14

The Composition

Furniture, lighting, art, and textiles are sourced — much of it custom or vintage, almost none from a catalog. Every piece is photographed in situ before it leaves the showroom.

Deliverables

  • FF&E specification & sourcing
  • Custom & vintage procurement
  • Art & object curation
  • Textile & soft-goods program
Showroom — Furniture review
05
Final 3 — 6 weeks

The Install

The studio is on-site daily. Pieces arrive, are placed, are moved, are placed again. The final week is spent styling — books, ceramics, the small objects that turn a finished house into yours.

Deliverables

  • White-glove install & placement
  • Final styling pass
  • Photography & documentation
  • Care & maintenance binder
Final styling — Day of handover

Twelve months from now,
you'll be home.
