The Designer

Ellada Mesveliani

Coming from Mediterranean influences, where light and tranquility define the landscape, I create interiors that mirror the soul's desire for serenity and balance.

My design philosophy celebrates minimalistic luxury, spaces that are clean, effortless, and deeply personal. I believe every environment holds an energy, a silent rhythm that should feel peaceful and alive.

As an architectural interior designer, my passion lies in custom design, crafting spaces that resonate with each person's inner world. Each project becomes a reflection of authenticity, defined elements, and timeless harmony. To me, creating interiors is more than design; it's the art of channeling love, beauty, and life into form.

Every detail tells a story of connection, simplicity, and human warmth, because true luxury is not in what you add… but in what you allow to be.

— Ellada
