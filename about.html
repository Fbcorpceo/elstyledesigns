§ AAbout the Practice

A studio of one,
and a careful few.

Portrait — Ellada Mesveliani, Los Angeles

Ellada Mesveliani founded El Style Designs in Los Angeles in 2019, after a decade designing interiors across Tbilisi, Milan, and New York.

Rooms that hold their composure.

The work is residential, mostly — single-family homes, pieds-à-terre, the occasional small hotel. Each project is treated as a long-form essay: researched in advance, drafted slowly, edited until what remains feels inevitable.

Ellada works hand-in-glove with a small bench of architects, makers, and tradespeople — almost all in California, several in Georgia and Italy — and personally walks every site, every showroom, and every install.

She is not interested in trends. She is interested in light, in materials that age well, and in how a house feels at six in the morning.

§ A.III — On the work

"I am not designing a room. I am writing the next chapter of someone's life, in materials that will be there for it."

i.

Material over motif

Honest materials, given room to age. Limewash, oak, soapstone, brass — never veneer, rarely chrome.

ii.

Restraint, then generosity

Edit until the room is empty. Add only what answers a question the room is asking.

iii.

The long view

Every choice tested against the question: will this be loved in twelve years, or only this one?

Let's compose
something quiet.
